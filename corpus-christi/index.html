      Religious Education (CCD)

      Youth & Family

      Youth Group for grades 6–12 meets Wednesdays at 5:30 PM in Marian Hall, right after CCD. There is also a Junior Youth Group (grades 1–5), Mom & Me (newborn to VPK), Parents’ Night Out and Vacation Bible School each summer.

      Michelle Cash-Chapman, Director of Religious Education & Youth Ministry · CCD-YouthMinistry@ccstaug.com

      Volunteering with children

      To protect children and those who serve them, the Diocese of St. Augustine requires a background check, fingerprinting and the “Protecting God’s Children” course before you begin. The parish will cover the fee if needed. Clearance can take up to four weeks.

      Please contact Michelle Cash-Chapman, our Safe Environment Coordinator, to begin.

      Since 1977

      A pilgrim church that found its home

      1. 1975

        Fr. William Mooney proposes a mission in St. Augustine Shores, served from St. Ambrose in Elkton.

      2. 1976

        Bishop Paul Tanner celebrates the Feast of Corpus Christi at the little mission in June.

      3. 1977

        Corpus Christi becomes a parish on November 14. Daily Mass moves into a rented garage on Phoenetia Drive, then the Riverview Club.

      4. 1981

        Bishop John Snyder dedicates the fan-shaped church on April 30.

      5. 1999

        Marian Hall is completed.

      6. 2017

        Fr. Mooney retires after forty years with the parish.

      7. 2022

        The parish welcomes Fr. Matthew Ibok.

      8. 2025

        Fr. Mooney, our founding pastor, goes home to the Lord on February 16.

      9. 2026

        New carpet and pews are blessed, and Bishop Erik Pohlmeier approves the new Administration Building.
